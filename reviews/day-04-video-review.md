## Corrections and limits

- The proposed [Traditional Chinese Actein video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MWpHMdyfCqg)
  describes a different brand's effervescent tablet and Actein powder. It
  was not linked to the Actein effervescent sheet. The other Actein videos
  have product or generic-technique notices.
- The Actein sheet's water instruction was changed in all seven languages
  to 60–100 mL drinking water, without requiring a particular temperature.
  Hospital references vary: [cool water](https://www.pohai.org.tw/drug_detail.php?PKey=127),
  [room-temperature water](https://www.cth.org.tw/public/medi_news/d0a380298c598b5bb68039fcd3c22743.pdf),
  and [25–37°C lukewarm water](https://www-ws.gov.taipei/001/Upload/public/Attachment/39161604531.pdf).
  The sheet still says not to use hot water and to wait until fully dissolved.
- Paxlovid videos show the usual three-tablet dose. The video page warns
  patients to follow their own blister pack and prescription because
  [renal-dose packs differ](https://www.paxlovid.com/how-to-take).
- The oseltamivir video shows the Taiwanese capsule-to-suspension process;
  foreign-language candidates for a commercial powder bottle or a
  single-capsule mixture were not linked.
