## Illustrations

All 29 current PNGs were downloaded from the four Drive `main` folders
using their `records.json` step IDs. Each is 1200 × 896 and has a distinct
SHA-256 hash. A contact sheet was visually checked against the existing
step text and order: five ordinary eye-drop steps and eight steps each for
special eye drops, eye ointment, and ear drops.

The ear-drop step after instillation depicts gentle pressure on the tragus,
while the existing text said to pull the earlobe. The seven-language step
note now describes gentle tragus pressure, consistent with
[MedlinePlus instructions](https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a601169.html)
and [Frimley Health NHS guidance](https://www.fhft.nhs.uk/patients-and-visitors/patient-information-library/how-use-ear-drops/submit/7561).

## Video routes

The four medicines have seven language-specific QR routes. Six languages
have a video for each eye-drop sheet; Traditional Chinese and English also
have eye-ointment and ear-drop videos.

| Sheet | Video languages | No suitable video |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Eye drops | zh-TW, en, id, vi, th, ja | tl |
| Special eye drops | zh-TW, en, id, vi, th, ja | tl |
| Eye ointment | zh-TW, en | id, vi, th, tl, ja |
| Ear drops | zh-TW, en | id, vi, th, tl, ja |

The 16 registry entries reuse nine distinct YouTube videos where the same
technique serves more than one sheet. YouTube oEmbed confirmed each video's
existence, title, and channel. The Drive candidate records supply the
spoken-language and content assessment; oEmbed alone does not verify
captions or every spoken instruction. Unsupported language combinations
display the existing explicit no-video message.

The English eye-drop video uses a general technique. The sheet remains the
source for any product-specific contact-lens and opening-date instructions.
The ear-drop illustration and sheet use the same adult/under-three ear
directions described in [MedlinePlus](https://medlineplus.gov/druginfo/meds/a601169.html).
