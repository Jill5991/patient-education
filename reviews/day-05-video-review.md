`main` folders. Six medicines' `records.json` files were also cross-checked;
the Benovate `records.json` was listed by Drive but returned 404 when fetched,
so its six image IDs were taken from the current folder listing. Each is 1200 × 896;
the files have distinct SHA-256 hashes. A contact sheet was checked against
each original step, including the actual redrawn images where the summary's
descriptive text does not match the final picture. The three sheets with
`內容待填寫` have no illustration and remain in clinical review.

## Excluded candidates and notices

- The proposed [Traditional Chinese Permethrin video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Tmalx-g_c94)
  is about Jaline lotion, which contains benzyl benzoate 25%, not permethrin
  5% cream. [Far Eastern Memorial Hospital's formulary](https://www.e-pharm.info/safety/drug-information/femh-formulary/item/ebbl15)
  and [Taipei Veterans General Hospital's scabies guidance](https://ihealth.vghtpe.gov.tw/media/569)
  distinguish the products and their application schedules. It was excluded.
- The proposed [Traditional Chinese adapalene video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9a2SBkpaaPo)
  discusses multiple topical retinoids and names Aklief and Epiduo in its
  published title. It does not provide an unambiguous demonstration of this
  single-agent adapalene 0.1% sheet, so it was excluded. The
  [Aklief label](https://www.aklief.com/sites/default/files/2023-06/Jan-2022-Aklief%20PI-MG%20FPI-0406.pdf)
  identifies trifarotene 0.005%, and the
  [FDA Epiduo label](https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/drugsatfda_docs/label/2026/220736Orig1s000lbl.pdf)
  identifies the adapalene/benzoyl peroxide combination.
- The [Traditional Chinese minoxidil video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=S5D-E2J3Zeg)
  demonstrates both solution and foam. The video page explicitly directs
  patients using this solution sheet to follow its 1 mL dose and ignore foam
  dosing.
- The English Benovate candidate is titled `Betnovate Scalp Application`.
  The video page notes that patients must follow their own bottle, sheet, and
  prescription for dose and frequency.
